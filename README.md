WELCOME TO JELL ZONE

Controls:

A/D : Move left/right
W : Float, if in midair
L: Jump!
; : Fire your weapon.
' : Change your weapon type.
P : Dash
L in midair: Buoy (float upwards!)


Menu Controls:

Press B while hovering the cursor over a button in a window to press it.
On the title screen, press SPACE to enter the level editor.


Level Editor:
Use WASD to move the camera.
Press P to place the player.

Press B over the entity and tile buttons on the right side of the screen to cycle through entities and tiles to place.

Press 1 to place an entity.
Press 2 to place a tile.
Press 3 to clear an entity or tile. You cannot clear the player.

Press ENTER to save your level to the file editor_level.json in maps.
template.json in maps is the level the editor loads on startup. To edit a level, rename it to template.json.
testlevel.json is the map the game loads when playing. To load a different map, rename it to testlevel.json.
testlevel_standard.json is the map the game switches to when touching a level switch entity. To load a different map, rename it to testlevel_standard.json.
